Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our healthcare services and procedures.

+ How do I register a new patient?

To register a new patient in our system, please follow these steps:

  1. Navigate to the 'Patients' section in the main menu
  2. Click the 'New Patient' button in the top-right corner
  3. Fill in all required fields (marked with an asterisk *)
  4. Upload any necessary documents (ID, insurance card, etc.)
  5. Review the information and click 'Save'

Note: All new patient registrations require verification of insurance and photo ID.

+ How do I schedule an appointment for a patient?

To schedule a new appointment:

  1. Go to the 'Appointments' section
  2. Click 'New Appointment'
  3. Select the patient from the dropdown or search by name/ID
  4. Choose the provider, date, and time
  5. Select the appointment type and reason
  6. Add any special notes if needed
  7. Click 'Schedule' to confirm

Tip: Use the calendar view to see provider availability in real-time.

+ How do I process an insurance claim?

To process an insurance claim:

  1. Navigate to the patient's billing section
  2. Select 'New Claim'
  3. Verify the patient's insurance information
  4. Enter the procedure codes and diagnosis codes
  5. Attach any required documentation
  6. Review and submit the claim

Note: Claims are typically processed within 7-10 business days.

+ What information do I need to update a patient's record?

When updating a patient's record, you may need:

  • Current contact information (phone, email, address)
  • Insurance details (policy number, group number)
  • Emergency contact information
  • Medical history updates
  • Current medications and allergies

Important: Always verify the patient's identity before making updates.

+ How do I reschedule or cancel an appointment?

To reschedule or cancel an appointment:

  1. Locate the appointment in the calendar view
  2. Click on the appointment to open details
  3. Select 'Reschedule' or 'Cancel'
  4. If rescheduling, choose a new date and time
  5. Add a reason for cancellation if required
  6. Confirm the changes

Note: Please notify patients of any cancellation policies at the time of scheduling.

+ What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods for your convenience:

  • Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover)
  • Health Savings Account (HSA) cards
  • Personal checks
  • Cash
  • Mobile payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay)

Note: Payment is due at the time of service unless prior arrangements have been made.

+ What should I do in case of a medical emergency?

In case of a medical emergency:

  1. Call emergency services (911) immediately
  2. Notify the front desk or nearest staff member
  3. Provide first aid if trained to do so
  4. Gather the patient's medical information if available
  5. Escort emergency personnel to the patient when they arrive

Important: Never attempt to move a seriously injured person unless absolutely necessary for their safety.

+ How do I verify a patient's insurance?

To verify a patient's insurance:

  1. Go to the patient's profile
  2. Select the 'Insurance' tab
  3. Click 'Verify Insurance'
  4. Enter the required information (member ID, group number, etc.)
  5. Click 'Verify' to check coverage

Note: Always verify insurance eligibility before the patient's appointment to avoid billing issues.

+ How do I access a patient's medical records?

To access a patient's medical records:

  1. Navigate to the 'Patients' section
  2. Search for the patient by name or ID
  3. Click on the patient's name to open their profile
  4. Select 'Medical Records' from the sidebar
  5. Choose the type of records you need to view

Note: Access to medical records is restricted based on your user role and permissions to ensure patient confidentiality.

+ How do I process a prescription refill request?

To process a prescription refill request:

  1. Navigate to the 'Prescriptions' section
  2. Select the patient's name
  3. Choose the medication that needs a refill
  4. Verify the prescription details (dosage, quantity, refills remaining)
  5. Select the preferred pharmacy or choose to print the prescription
  6. Click 'Submit' to process the refill

Note: Please allow 24-48 hours for prescription refills to be processed.

+ How can I check a patient's billing history?

To view a patient's billing history:

  1. Go to the 'Billing' section
  2. Search for the patient by name or account number
  3. Click on 'Billing History'
  4. Filter by date range, status, or amount if needed
  5. Click on any entry to view detailed information

Tip: You can print statements or export the data for record-keeping.

+ What are the emergency protocols for the reception area?

In case of an emergency in the reception area:

  1. Remain calm and assess the situation
  2. If medical attention is needed, call the emergency extension (or 911 if external help is required)
  3. Alert the nearest healthcare provider
  4. Clear the area if necessary for emergency personnel
  5. Follow the instructions of the healthcare provider or emergency personnel
  6. Document the incident according to facility policy

Important: Emergency contact numbers and protocols are posted at each reception desk.
